BHRIGU NANDI NADI (BNN) — COMPLETE RULES
Source: "Bhrigu Nandi Nadi — An Astrology That Really Predicts" by Vaibhav Gupta
Compiled from Bhrigu Rishi + Nandikeshwar Rishi traditions, modernised by RG Rao (1950s)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT MAKES BNN UNIQUE (vs Vedic/Lal Kitab)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. NO Lagna/Ascendant used — because birth time may be wrong
2. NO Dasha system used — because Moon degree can change
3. Uses ONLY the Rashi (D1) chart — no divisional charts needed
4. Jupiter's position = 1st House for every chart (Jupiter = Jeeva Karaka = Self)
5. Houses counted ANTICLOCKWISE from Jupiter's position
6. Rahu/Ketu do NOT use 7th house aspect rule (unlike other planets)
7. 90% of predictions come from planets and their combinations alone

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
RULE 1 — JUPITER AS FIRST HOUSE (Most Important Rule)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Wherever Jupiter sits in the birth chart = that Rashi becomes House 1
• Count anticlockwise from Jupiter's Rashi for all 12 houses
• Example: Jupiter in Sagittarius (Rashi 9) → 9=H1, 10=H2, 11=H3, 12=H4, 1=H5...
• Example: Jupiter in Libra (Rashi 7) → 7=H1, 8=H2, 9=H3, 10=H4...
• WHY Jupiter? Jupiter is Prana Shakti / Life Energy = Jeev Karaka = the Self
• Jupiter's Rashi characteristics = the person's personality and appearance

RULE 2 — TRINAL RASHIS = PLANETARY COMBINATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Planets in TRINAL RASHIS are considered in COMBINATION (as if conjunct)
The 4 Trinal groups (forming triangles in the chart):
  GROUP 1: Rashis 1, 5, 9  (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius — Fire signs)
  GROUP 2: Rashis 2, 6, 10 (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn — Earth signs)
  GROUP 3: Rashis 3, 7, 11 (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius — Air signs)
  GROUP 4: Rashis 4, 8, 12 (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces — Water signs)

HOW TO FORM A COMBINATION — 3 methods:
  a) Planets in the SAME Rashi (direct conjunction)
  b) Planets in TRINAL Rashis (e.g., Planet A in Aries + Planet B in Leo = combo)
  c) Planet in the NEXT Rashi to another planet (adjacent sign = combo)
  d) Planet in the 7TH Rashi from another planet (opposition = combo)
     EXCEPTION: Rahu and Ketu do NOT use the 7th house rule

PREDICTION METHOD:
  → Join the Karakatwas (significations) of all planets in combination
  → The combined meaning = the prediction
  → Study in pairs: if 3 planets in combo, read them 2 at a time

HOW TO READ A BNN CHART — STEP BY STEP
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 1: Find Jupiter's Rashi → This becomes BNN House 1
STEP 2: Count anticlockwise to assign BNN houses 1-12
STEP 3: Identify ALL planetary combinations using:
         - Same sign (direct conjunction)
         - Trinal signs (1,5,9 | 2,6,10 | 3,7,11 | 4,8,12)
         - Adjacent sign (next sign anticlockwise)
         - 7th sign opposition (except Rahu/Ketu)
STEP 4: For each combination, join the Karakatwas of both/all planets
STEP 5: Read the combined meaning as the life event/trait
STEP 6: Check the Dispositor of each key planet for strength
STEP 7: Jupiter's Rashi = personality of the native
STEP 8: Give BNN-based remedies from the combination

BNN vs LAL KITAB — KEY DIFFERENCES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
BNN: House 1 = wherever Jupiter sits
LK:  House 1 = always Aries (fixed)

BNN: No Dasha used, no Lagna used
LK:  Has its own 35-year cycle; Lagna considered when birth time known

BNN: Predictions from planetary combinations and Karakatwas
LK:  Predictions from planet-house placements, Karak, enemy analysis

BNN: Remedies activate combinations (sun bath, wear wristwatch, donate food)
LK:  Remedies neutralise malefic planets (grains, copper coins, donations)

BNN: Trinal + adjacent + 7th house = combination
LK:  Fixed house position + Karak enemies determine results

USE BOTH TOGETHER: BNN for event prediction and personality; LK for karmic debts, remedies and house signification analysis
