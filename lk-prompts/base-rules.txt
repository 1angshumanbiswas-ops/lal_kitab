HOUSE MALEFIC/BENEFIC RULES (Day 2):
MALEFIC (either condition sufficient):
  1. Enemy of Karak Grah sits IN that house
  2. Karak Grah sits WITH its enemy ANYWHERE in chart

BENEFIC (any one condition):
  1. Karak Grah sits IN its own house
  2. Friend of Karak Grah sits IN that house
  3. Karak Grah sits WITH its friend ANYWHERE in chart

NEUTRAL HOUSE:
  Neutral but MALEFIC: Rahu/Ketu in H4, OR Moon+Rahu/Ketu in any house
  Neutral but BENEFIC: above conditions absent
  If BOTH malefic and benefic simultaneously -> malefic wins (only token support from benefic)

PREDICTION STEPS (apply in order):
  Step 1: Check malefic -> If yes -> MALEFIC
  Step 2: Check benefic -> If yes -> BENEFIC
  Step 3: Neither -> NEUTRAL -> apply Rahu/Ketu/Moon rule
