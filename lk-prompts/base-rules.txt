PREDICTIVE RULES — HOUSE NATURE (Mahesh Mankar Day 2)
════════════════════════════════════════
Every house is MALEFIC, BENEFIC, or NEUTRAL.

MALEFIC — either condition triggers it:
1. Enemy of Karak Grah sits IN that house
   e.g. H1 Karak=Sun. Rahu in H1 → H1 malefic (Rahu is Sun's enemy)
2. Karak Grah sits WITH its enemy ANYWHERE in chart
   e.g. H2 Karak=Jupiter. Jupiter+Mercury conjunct anywhere → H2 malefic
RULE: Once malefic → PERMANENTLY malefic. Cannot become benefic.
PREDICTION: Problems in that house's life areas.

BENEFIC — any one condition:
1. Karak Grah sits IN its own house
   e.g. H5 Karak=Jupiter. Jupiter in H5 → benefic
2. Friend of Karak Grah sits IN that house
   e.g. H7 Karak=Venus. Mercury (Venus friend) in H7 → benefic
3. Karak Grah sits WITH friend ANYWHERE in chart
   e.g. H11 Karak=Jupiter. Jupiter+Mars conjunct anywhere → H11 benefic
PREDICTION: Positive results from that house.

NEUTRAL HOUSE RULES:
→ Neutral but MALEFIC: Rahu/Ketu in H4, OR Moon+Rahu/Ketu in any house
  (Native doesn't get full results, feels something missing)
→ Neutral but BENEFIC: above condition absent
  (Native gets moderate results, feels satisfied)

PREDICTION STEPS (always apply in this order):
Step 1: Check malefic → If yes → MALEFIC
Step 2: If not malefic → Check benefic → If yes → BENEFIC
Step 3: Neither → NEUTRAL
Step 4: For neutral → check Rahu/Ketu/Moon rule for real nature
